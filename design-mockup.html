Frequently Asked Questions
FAQ
Common Questions
The Answers.
  • Isn't this just anti-Islamic propaganda?
    Every entry is sourced to Muslim-approved texts. The arguments are philosophical, historical, and textual — not rhetorical. If a source is incorrect, it can be verified and corrected. Propaganda doesn't cite its sources.
  • Why use only Sunni sources?
    The catalog focuses on mainstream Sunni Islam — the largest Muslim denomination globally, with the most developed canonical textual corpus. Using the sources Sunni jurisprudence treats as authoritative eliminates the objection that we're using fringe texts.
  • What does "Strong" on an entry mean?
    Strong entries are those where the apologetic response itself generates a deeper problem, or where answering the critique requires abandoning a core Islamic claim. They're the arguments most resistant to standard deflection techniques.
  • Is a Muslim response included?
    Where a standard apologetical response exists, many entries include it and address it directly. The goal is to engage the strongest version of the Muslim position, not misrepresent it.
  • Do I need an account to use the site?
    No. The catalog, all readers, and the compare tool are open to everyone. An account is only required for the Build tool — saving collections, writing private notes, and sharing dossiers.
  • Can I cite entries from this site?
    Every entry cites its primary source. You should cite the primary source directly — not this site. The entry gives you the reference; the reader lets you verify it. Verify before citing.
  • How is entry strength rated?
    Entries are rated Basic, Moderate, or Strong based on how resistant the argument is to standard apologetical responses. Strong entries require abandoning a core Islamic claim to refute.
