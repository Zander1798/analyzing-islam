Volume I · A Critical Reference Guide
A structured reference guide to Islam's most authoritative source texts — the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari, and Sahih Muslim. Each entry presents a specific verse, hadith, or doctrine exactly as it appears in the primary text, examines its context and significance, surveys the standard Muslim apologetic response, and explains precisely where that response falls short of critical scrutiny.
Entries organized by source and chapter across all three volumes
Primary-text quotations drawn directly from authoritative editions
Strength ratings — Basic, Moderate, Strong — for each argument
Cross-referenced General Index, Quran Verse Index, and Hadith Reference Index
Designed for researchers, apologists, and informed general readers
