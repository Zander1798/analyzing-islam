Arabic & Islamic terminology
Ayah (pl. Ayat)A verse of the Quran
SurahA chapter of the Quran; 114 total
Hadith (pl. Ahadith)A recorded saying or action of the Prophet Muhammad
SunnahThe normative practice of the Prophet, derived from hadiths
IsnadThe chain of transmission of a hadith
MatnThe text body of a hadith (as distinct from its isnad)
SahihAuthentic — the highest grade of hadith reliability
HasanGood — a reliable hadith, one grade below Sahih
Da'ifWeak — a hadith with a deficient chain or text

NaskhAbrogation — the doctrine that later verses cancel earlier ones
TafsirQuranic exegesis / commentary
FiqhIslamic jurisprudence
UlemaIslamic scholars and jurists collectively
DhimmiA non-Muslim subject living under Islamic rule, subject to special taxes and restrictions
HududFixed Quranic punishments — amputation, stoning, lashing — that cannot be reduced by a judge
JinnSupernatural beings made of smokeless fire; part of Islamic cosmology, capable of possession
RuqyaQuranic recitation used as spiritual healing or exorcism against jinn and the evil eye
Mut'ahTemporary marriage contract, permitted in early Islam and still practiced in Shia tradition
MahramA male relative a woman cannot marry — her guardian for travel under Islamic law
FitraThe innate human nature Islam claims all people are born with — naturally inclined to Islam
JizyaA tax levied on non-Muslims living under Islamic governance in lieu of military service
DajjalThe Islamic Antichrist figure; a one-eyed deceiver who appears before the Day of Judgment
TahrifThe Islamic claim that Jews and Christians corrupted their scriptures
