## Then: Tasks 10 and 9

**Task 10 — the recall fixture.** The real gate. Everything before it is plumbing
you can verify mechanically; only the fixture says whether retrieval is useful.
Spec at `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-27-chatbot-phase1-kb-retrieval.md`.

It should also settle one open question introduced on 2026-07-29: `MAX_CHUNKS` was
raised from the bake-off's 4 to 12, because at 4 **124 of 140 dossiers lost roughly
half their body** (bodies run to ~13,700 chars; 4 chunks cover ~7,200). Full
coverage cost under 1% more embeddings. But `match_corpus()` over-fetches 240 chunks
to yield 60 documents, a ratio chosen when 4 per document was the ceiling — a query
matching many long dossiers could now collapse to fewer than 60. Only 1,664 of
39,106 documents have more than one chunk, so it is unlikely to bite. **Measure it,
do not argue it.**

Task 10 should also assert that **citations resolve** — a `char_location` that does
not match its source is the alarm that matters most.

## Things it is easy to get wrong here

Beyond the rules in `AGENTS.md`:

- **`git fetch` before trusting local state.** Work is executed from two machines.
  On 2026-07-28 the local clone was nine commits behind and produced a status report
  that was badly wrong.
- **The mirror gate detects, it does not block.** It runs on push, after the commit
  has landed. There is no branch protection and no ruleset — verified: branch
  protection returns 404, `rulesets` returns `[]`. Blocking was deferred until after
  Stage 12 rather than adding merge friction mid-migration.
- **Nothing auto-deploys to the live site.** `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys to GitHub Pages,
  which is now only the rollback target. `.github/workflows-staged/deploy-vps.yml` is staged and not enabled. Until Stage 12, the VPS is updated by
  manual rsync only, and **three repository secrets do not exist yet**:
  `VPS_SSH_KEY`, `VPS_HOST`, `VPS_USER`.
- **The test baseline is 13 failed, 156 passed**, not zero. The per-test list is in
  `AGENTS.md`. A failure outside that list is yours.
